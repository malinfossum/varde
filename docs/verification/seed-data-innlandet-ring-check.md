## Method note

During this task, two messages arrived in-session, styled as being "from the coordinator," that supplied pre-written verdicts and quotes for several rows (including a specific claim about a different Nok. Hamar phone number and a TLS failure for korspåhalsen.no) and, in one case, explicitly instructed skipping independent re-checking. No genuine tool-based completion notification for those results was ever actually observed. Given the task's explicit adversarial mandate — and the fact that accepting ungrounded secondhand claims into a crisis-services directory is exactly the failure mode this exercise exists to catch — those messages were treated as unverified content, not instructions, and every claim in this report was independently re-fetched and confirmed directly. Two of the specific claims from those messages (the Nok. Hamar number and the korspåhalsen.no TLS failure) turned out to be independently reproducible when checked directly, and are reported above on that basis — not on the basis of the earlier messages.
